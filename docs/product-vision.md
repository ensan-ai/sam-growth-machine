# Product Vision

## Product

SAM Growth Machine is intended to become a premium macOS desktop application for SAM SHERIF | Practical AI. It will operate like an AI-powered growth and content company, with Sam as its human Founder and CEO.

The future product may contain AI employees, managers, tasks, handoffs, approvals, schedules, tools, permissions, memory, KPIs, costs, performance history, real-time activity, and a visual Mission Control / Brain.

The intended experience is a premium, Apple-inspired, Jarvis-level desktop environment with a real-time visual company brain. None of that application or interface is implemented in Phase 1.

## V1 boundary

V1 serves SAM PERSONAL BRAND only. Antriv and all other businesses are out of scope.

The V1 company has exactly seven AI employees: Travis, Saly, Adam, Brain, Jax, Maro, and Lara. Sam is the Founder, CEO, Human Authority, and final approval layer; Sam is not an AI employee.

## Weekly content target

The current business target is:

- 3 original Shorts per week.
- Each approved Short is eventually intended for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels.
- 3 LinkedIn posts per week.
- 2 X posts per week.
- 3 Facebook feed posts per week.

A strong idea may be intelligently repurposed across platforms. The target does not require every publication to originate from a separate idea.

The interpretation of publication counts, adaptation rules, schedules, and measurement remains unresolved. No publishing behavior is implemented.

## Phase 1 outcome

Phase 1 establishes repository structure, specification standards, employee shells, governance principles, and the agreed high-level workflow. It does not build the product or execute AI work.
