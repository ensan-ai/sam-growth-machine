## Reporting is not workflow

The hierarchy above defines formal management relationships only. Workflow handoffs are separate: an employee may receive work from or send work to another employee without either employee being the other's manager.

The sequence in the growth loop must not be used to infer receives_from, sends_to, direct collaborators, or additional management responsibilities. Those fields remain unresolved for Phase 2.
